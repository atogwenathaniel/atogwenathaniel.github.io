Privacy Policy

Knightfall Chess — last updated October 2026

Summary

Knightfall Chess is designed to work offline. Your games, settings, and statistics are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not have accounts or logins. The app displays advertising through Google AdMob to support development; the AdMob SDK collects certain device and advertising identifiers as described below.

What we collect

We, the developers of Knightfall Chess, do not collect or store any personal information about you. We have no backend servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

What is stored on your device

Your saved games, player names, puzzle history, and app settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

Advertising

Knightfall Chess displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use certain information, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (AAID)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and approximate location (country level)
  • Information about ad interactions

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For details, please see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies overview.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Settings under "Ads". You can also purchase "Remove Ads" inside the app to disable advertising entirely.

Google Play Billing

If you purchase "Remove Ads", the transaction is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google handles all billing details according to its own privacy policy.

Network access

The app uses network access only to load advertisements from Google AdMob and to process in-app purchases through Google Play. No other network requests are made. All chess engine computation happens locally on your device.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

No other third-party services are used. The app does not include analytics, crash reporting, or social SDKs.

Children's privacy

Knightfall Chess is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
